Thermodynamics | R-22 Refrigeration Cycle Analysis

Analysed an R-22 vapour compression cycle across three test conditions (COP 1.46–1.98) using TESTA and first-law energy balances. Built a MATLAB model over condenser temperatures of 300–330 K, calibrating a reduced-order Carnot efficiency model to the experimental data and validating COP and compressor power trends.

R-22蒸気圧縮冷凍サイクルを3条件で解析（COP 1.46〜1.98）。TESTAと熱力学第一法則で評価。MATLABで凝縮温度300〜330 Kのシミュレーションを行い、実験データで簡易カルノー効率モデルを校正し、COPと圧縮機動力の傾向を検証。
